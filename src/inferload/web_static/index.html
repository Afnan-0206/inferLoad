About InferLoad

InferLoad is an LLM inference load testing and capacity analysis system. It subjects inference endpoints to closed-loop or open-loop synthetic workloads across concurrency sweeps, recording TTFT, inter-token latency, total latency, and throughput, and compares them against Service Level Objectives (SLOs) to determine defensible operational capacity.
